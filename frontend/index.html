About EcoLoop

ABOUT THE CREATOR

Hi, I’m Udit Narayan Tiwari, a student creator who enjoys building practical digital projects that make everyday choices clearer.

EcoLoop brings together smart waste management, web development and sustainability so households and students can keep useful materials in the loop.

View my GitHub ↗

THE ECOLOOP MISSION

EcoLoop is a simple guide for smarter waste management and more mindful resource recovery.

The goal is to make sorting easier and more useful—helping people reduce, reuse and recycle everyday materials with confidence.

MISSIONMake practical circular choices easy to understand.
VISIONSupport cleaner, more resource-efficient neighbourhoods.
IMPACTTurn small daily actions into lasting habits.
